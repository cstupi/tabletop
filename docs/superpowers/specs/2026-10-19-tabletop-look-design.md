# Tabletop look

Owner request, 2026-10-19: the table should read as a painted wargaming table — printed gaming mats, painted hobby terrain,
a well-lit room — not a flat-coloured voxel render. Owner rulings on the first pass: terrain detail is geometry, not texture
on smooth shapes ("rocks are not smooth, ruins are definitely not smooth at all"; "trees have leaves, not green blobs").
Rules data does not change: every catalog entry keeps its kind, footprint, top and declarations, and every mesh still meets
`scripts/TerrainMeshes/AGENTS.md` → Rules the meshes keep.

## Boards

The catalog's `textures` are gaming-mat prints. Each is `client/content/builtin/textures/<name>.png`, 1024 × 1024, one
repeat per 12" (85 px/in), tileable on both axes: RGB is the albedo (sRGB), A is the height (0 low, 255 high). A PNG
without alpha reads its height from its luminance. Generated by `scripts/TableTextures` (deterministic C#, no packages).

| Name | Mat |
| --- | --- |
| `grass` | Grassland: mixed green and straw grass, worn dirt paths and patches, small stones |
| `desert` | Desert: rippled ochre sand, wind streaks, scattered pebbles and dark grit |
| `urban` | Urban ruins: cracked rockcrete slabs on a grid, plate seams, rubble grit, oil and burn stains |
| `snow` | Tundra: wind-packed snow over dark frozen earth and rock showing through |
| `ash` | Ash world: grey-black volcanic ash, cinder grit, dark cooled-lava cracks |
| `wasteland` | Wasteland: red-brown rocky dust, cracked dry earth plates, scattered stones |
| `alien` | Alien world: violet-grey soil, teal mineral crusts, pale crystalline grit |

Existing layouts and games name the first five; the two new ones are added to the catalog's list.

The client draws the mat with `client/content/shaders/board.gdshader`:
- Normal map from the height (`HeightNormals`, client Core: central differences, wrapping, tangent space), built once per
  texture.
- Anti-tiling: a second sample of the same print at 2.7× the repeat, rotated, blended in by a low-frequency value noise
  through a height blend (the higher layer wins), and a ±8 % brightness and warmth drift at 30" scale.
- Roughness 0.8–0.95 from the height (low hollows smoother), no metal.

The slab is a 1"-thick painted board edge (the mat's mean colour darkened), on a dark wooden table that runs 40" past the
board and fades to the clear colour (`client/content/table/wood.png`, same generator).

## Terrain

Built-in meshes keep vertex colours as the paint and gain:
- **Geometry first.** Faceted, fractured, irregular; flat shading and hard edges where real terrain has them.
  - Rocks and cliffs: noise-carved, faceted, with strata ledges, chips and cracks; scree of individual chunks at the foot.
  - Ruins and buildings: jagged broken wall tops, missing and displaced blocks, per-brick or per-panel relief, broken window
    frames, exposed rebar, rubble piles of individual chunks; chamfered edges on every block.
  - Trees and vegetation: trunks with branching limbs, foliage as many small leaf cards and clusters (double-sided),
    tufts and shrubs on a flocked base.
  - Hills, craters, fields, water banks: flocked ground with tufts, stones and broken edges, not smooth domes.
- **The painter's finish** (`Bake`, at bake time): a warm-brown wash darkening recesses by ambient occlusion (cosine
  rays against the model and the table, 1.6" reach) and a pale drybrush on convex edges a brush-width wide.
- **Surface grain** (client, `TerrainLook`): each built-in surface gets a world-space triplanar detail texture by family
  (`SurfaceFamily`, client Core, from the glTF material name): stone, ground, foliage, wood, metal; water and smog keep
  their own. The detail multiplies the vertex colour around 1 and adds its normal map; roughness matte (0.85–0.95), metal
  0.45. Detail textures are `client/content/table/detail-<family>.png` (512², RGB grain, A height), packed in the client.

## Lighting

`TableLighting` (client) for the table scene:
- Key: warm white (1.0, 0.93, 0.82), energy 1.5, 52° down, from the near-left; shadows on, PSSM 4 splits, 220" max
  distance, blur 1.5, angular distance 1° (soft contact-hardening under Forward+).
- Fill: cool (0.62, 0.72, 1.0), energy 0.3, from the far-right, no shadow.
- Environment: background stays the theme's clear colour; ambient from a procedural sky (warm ground, cool top) at 0.55;
  reflections from the same sky; AgX tone mapping, exposure 1.0; SSAO radius 1", intensity 1.6 (Forward+ and Mobile; the
  baked wash carries the compatibility renderer); glow off; a 4 % saturation lift.
- The compatibility renderer (`--rendering-driver opengl3`) draws the same scene without SSAO and contact-hardening.

## Budgets

Built-in content under 60 MB packed: meshes at most 50 000 triangles and 3 MB each and 36 MB together
(`Budget`, `scripts/TerrainMeshes`); textures at most 3 MB each (`scripts/TableTextures`).
