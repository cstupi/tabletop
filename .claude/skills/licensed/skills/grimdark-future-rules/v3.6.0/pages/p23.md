# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 23

ASYMMETRIC DEPLOYMENT STYLES
These styles are meant to provide an unbalanced experience, with
one player being surrounded or disadvantaged.
To select a random deployment style, simply roll one die:
1. Open Warzone
2. Pushback
3. Cornered
4. Encircled
5. Behind Enemy Lines
6. Lightning Strike
1. OPEN WARZONE
DIAGRAMS: measured on the page images' 6" grid (72"×48" = 12×8 squares; the diagrams carry no inch labels); landscape table, long edges top and bottom.
Diagram — Open Warzone: zone A is an 18" strip along the top long edge; zone B is a 6" strip along the bottom long edge.
2. PUSHBACK
Diagram — Pushback: zone A is a 12" strip along the left short edge; zone B is a 36" strip along the right short edge.
3. CORNERED
Diagram — Cornered: zone A is a 24"×24" square in the bottom-left corner; zone B is an L-shape of a 12" strip along the top edge and a 24" strip along the right edge.
4. ENCIRCLED
Diagram — Encircled: zone A is 6" strips along the top, left and right edges (open along the bottom); zone B is a 24"×24" square on the bottom edge, centred, 18" in from each side strip and 18" below the top strip.
5. BEHIND ENEMY LINES
Diagram — Behind Enemy Lines: zone B is strips along the top edge (6"), the right edge (12") and the bottom edge (6"); zone A is a 48"×12" rectangle against the left edge, 12" below the top strip, 12" above the bottom strip and 12" short of the right strip.
6. LIGHTNING STRIKE
Diagram — Lightning Strike: zone A is two corner triangles on the left edge (top-left and bottom-left), each with 24" along its long edge and 24" along the left edge, meeting at the left edge's midpoint; zone B is a chevron filling the right side, its apex 24" from the left edge's midpoint and its sides meeting the top and bottom edges 24" from the right corners.
