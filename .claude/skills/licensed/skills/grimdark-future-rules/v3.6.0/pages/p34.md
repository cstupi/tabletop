# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 34

PLAYING WITH ADVANCED CASTING
Advanced Casting rules add more complexity to how casters work
in the game, making them more important to the battle.
Before the game, players can agree to use any or all of the
following in the game:
• Currents of Power
• Living Spells
• Relic Wargear
CURRENTS OF POWER
When playing with Currents of Power, the Caster(X) rule is heavily
modified, with players agreeing to use any or all of the following
systems:
• Spell Books
• 2D6 Casting
• Magic Pools
• Heavy Backlash
SPELL BOOKS
Before the game begins, for each Caster(X) roll to get X+1 random
spells from their spell list, and then pick X of those spells to be part
of that caster’s spell book for the battle.
2D6 Casting: Spells from a caster’s spell books are cast on a 7+ as
usual, whilst spells that are not part of their book are only
successfully cast on a 10+ instead.
Magic Pools: Spells from a caster’s spell books are cast on a 4+ as
usual, whilst spells that are not part of their book are only
successfully cast on a 6+ instead.
2D6 CASTING
This system can’t be used at the same time as Magic Pools.
Casting Spells: When casting spells, instead of rolling a single die
trying to score 4+ to resolve spell effects, players roll two dice
trying to score 7+ to resolve spell effects. If the result on both dice
is the same, then the caster suffers a backlash, losing 1 spell token
and taking 3 wounds, and after resolving any spell effects, the
caster can’t cast any more spells until the end of the round. Note
that players may spend spell tokens as usual to give the caster +1/-
1 to the roll.
Modifiers: When using 2D6 Casting, regardless of modifiers, results
of 12 always succeed, and results of 2 always fail (instead of always
succeeding on 6 and always failing on 1).
MAGIC POOLS
This system can’t be used at the same time as 2D6 Casting.
The Magic Pool: At the beginning of each round, each player gets
2*X spell dice in their magic pool, where X is the total number of
spell tokens all their casters would have generated that round. At
the end of each round, each player must discard unspent spell dice
down to 6*X dice, where X is the total number of casters in their
army.
Casting Spells: When casting spells, instead of rolling a single die
and spending spell tokens to get +1 to the roll, players may take
any number of spell dice from their magic pool, and roll them
instead. If you roll as many 4+ as the spell’s casting value, then the
spell is cast successfully. If any of the dice has an unmodified result
of 6, then the spell can’t be blocked. If any of the dice has an
unmodified result of 1, then the caster suffers a backlash, losing 1
spell die and taking 1 wound, and after resolving any spell effects,
the caster can’t cast any more spells until the end of the round.
Blocking Spells: When blocking spells, instead of spending spell
tokens to give -1 to the roll, players may take any number of spell
dice from their magic pool, and roll them at the same time as the
caster instead. Then, sum together the total result of all casting
dice, and the total result of all blocking dice. If the total blocking
result is equal or higher than the casting result, then the spell is
blocked, and its effects are not resolved. Note that if any of the
casting dice has an unmodified result of 6, then the spell can’t be
blocked
HEAVY BACKLASH
When using 2D6 Casting or Magic Pools, when a caster suffers a
backlash, roll 2D6 to see what happens, instead of losing 1 spell
token/die and taking 1 wound:
Result Backlash Effect
2-4 Cascade - The caster loses 1 spell token/die, takes
D3 wounds, and the caster’s unit takes 2D3 wounds,
or if the caster is not part of a unit the closest unit
within 6” takes 2D3 wounds.
5-6 Detonation - The caster loses 1 spell token/die and
takes D3 wounds.
7 Boom! - The caster loses 1 spell token/die and takes
1 wound.
8-9 Feedback - The caster loses D3 spell tokens/dice
and takes 1 wound.
10-12 Drained - The caster loses D3 spell tokens/dice,
takes 1 wound, and lowers its caster level by 1.
Note that casters that suffer a backlash can’t cast any more spells
until the end of the round.
