# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 64

3. CHECK FOR CASUALTIES
Casualties: At the end of the game, all units that were fully
destroyed or Routed count as casualties for this mission.
Regular Units: For every unit that is a casualty roll one die to see
what happens:
Result Casualty
1 Dead - Remove the unit from your army sheet
(losing all its points).
2-5 Recovered - Unit recovers and may be used without
penalties.
6 Natural Talent - Unit recovers and earns +1 XP.
Hero Units: Follow the same rules as above, but on a 1 it counts as
being 25pts cheaper and gets an Injury trait, and on a 6 it counts as
being 25pts more expensive and gains a Talent trait.
Result Injury Trait
1 Dead - Remove the Hero from your army sheet
(losing all its points).
2 Weakened - Gets -1 to Defense rolls when blocking
hits.
3 Maimed - Gets -1 to hit rolls when fighting in melee.
4 Blinded - Gets -1 to hit rolls when using move
actions and shooting.
5 Crippled - Moves -1” on Advance and -2” on
Rush/Charge actions.
6 Lucky Break - May pick any other trait from the list.
Result Talent Trait
1 Natural Talent - Earns +1 XP.
2 Motivated - Gets +1 to rolls when taking Morale
Tests.
3 Crazed - Gets +1 to hit rolls in melee when charging.
4 Vigilant - Gets +1 to hit rolls when shooting.
5 Toughened - Gets Tough(+3).
6 Learned Lesson - May pick any other trait from the
list.
Each trait (except for Natural Talent) can only be gained once, so if
you roll a trait you already have keep re-rolling until you get a new
trait you don’t have yet.
No Permadeath: Optionally you can play without units being
removed from the army when they die, but instead they lose all XP
earned during the match and must sit out the next D3 matches.
4. EARN EXPERIENCE
Earning XP: Units earn XP in battle for each of the following things:
• +1 XP: Not a casualty
• +1 XP: Killed a unit
• +2 XP: Killed a Hero unit
• +3 XP: Killed a Hero and its unit
Mark earned XP on the army sheet in order to see when units level
up. Note that Heroes attached to units also earn XP with the unit.
Regular Units: When a unit earns 5 XP it counts as being 25pts
more expensive and you roll one die to see what trait it gets:
Result Trait
1 Agile - Moves +1” on Advance and +2” on
Rush/Charge actions.
2 Headstrong - Gets +1 to rolls when taking Morale
Tests.
3 Specialist - Gets +1 to hit rolls when in melee or
when shooting (pick one).
4 Resilient - Gets +1 to Defense rolls when blocking
hits.
5 Elite - May re-roll one roll of any kind once per
game.
6 Fast Learner - May pick any other trait from the list.
Each trait (except for Specialist) can only be gained once, so if you
roll a trait you already have keep re-rolling until you get a new trait
you don’t have yet. Units can’t earn more than 30xp, so they can
level up max. 6 times per campaign.
Hero Units: Follow the same rules as above, but Heroes count as
being 55pts more expensive (instead of only 25pts) when they level
up and get skill traits on top of regular traits. The first time a Hero
levels up roll one die and pick one skill set:
Result Skill Set
1-2 Pathfinder or Captain
3-4 Warrior or Hunter
5-6 Warden or Overseer
Every time the Hero levels up, including the first time, roll one die
to see what skill trait it gets from its skill set. Each trait can only be
gained once, so if you roll a trait you already have keep re-rolling
until you get a new trait you don’t have yet. If a Hero already has all
3 traits of its skill set, then it may roll on the table again to get a
new second skill set.
