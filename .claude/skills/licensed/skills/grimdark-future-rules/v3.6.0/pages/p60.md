# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 60

SPECIAL RULES
General Special Rules: AI units must always use their special rules
as soon as they are activated, targeting the closest unit that makes
sense.
Ambush: AI units with Ambush must always be kept in reserve, and
must always deploy at the start of the second round.
Artillery: AI units with Artillery must deploy in the highest position
with most line of sight of the table as possible regardless of
distance from objectives.
Blast: AI units with Blast weapons always target multi-model units
without Tough first, and multi-model units with Tough second.
Caster: AI units always cast spells after moving, selecting a random
spell by rolling D3+X, where X is their caster level. If there is no valid
target for that spell, or they don’t have enough tokens to cast it,
they must cycle through the list until there is a valid spell, or else
don’t cast anything.
Deadly: AI units with Deadly weapons always target single-model
units with Tough first, and multi-model units with Tough second.
Indirect: AI units with Indirect weapons that are in range of
enemies always use Hold actions and shoot.
Relentless: AI units with Relentless that have weapons that are in
range of enemies always use Hold and shoot.
Scout: AI units with Scout must always be deployed after all other
units, following the AI deployment rules.
Transport: The AI must always place random units in each
Transport, trying to fill up their cargo limit. AI units with Transport
always activate before their cargo on the first round, and units
inside of them must always disembark on their first activation (if
possible).
AI DECISION TREES
When it’s the AI’s turn to activate a unit, follow these steps:
1. Is this unit within 3” of an objective that is not controlled by
the AI?
• Yes - Go to step 2
• No - Go to step 3
2. Is any enemy in range to shoot/charge without moving this
unit over 3” away from the objective?
• Yes - Advance and shoot closest, or charge closest
• No - Hold position
3. Is there any objective not controlled by the AI that has no
non-Shaken AI units within 3” of it?
• Yes - Go to step 4
• No - Go to step 5
4. If you move towards the objective, will any enemy be in
range to shoot/charge?
• Yes - Advance towards objective and shoot closest, or Charge
closest towards objective if you can deal more damage than
with shooting
• No - Rush toward objective
5. Is any enemy in range to shoot/charge?
• Yes - Advance and shoot closest, or charge closest
• No - Rush toward closest objective
