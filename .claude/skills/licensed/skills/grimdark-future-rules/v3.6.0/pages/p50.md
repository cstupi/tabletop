# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 50

BOARDING ACTIONS SCENARIOS
When playing Boarding Actions, players must first set up the table
to resemble the interior of a battle ship or space station, but
otherwise the game is set up normally.
Before starting, divide the table into 12”x12” squares, and then for
each full square take D3+4 pieces of wall terrain (about 3”-6” long).
Wall terrain counts as Impassable and Sight Blocking terrain, and
can’t be climbed or moved over in any way.
The players must roll-off, and then alternate in picking one table
square each, and placing walls. Before placing the walls, the player
must roll one die, and on a 1-3 they must select 1 wall to have a
hatchway (about 1”-2” wide), and on a 4-6 they must select 2 walls
to have a hatchway instead. Then the player must place all walls
fully within their table square, before alternating to the next player.
Walls must be placed like this:
• Each wall must be connected to at least one other wall
• Walls must be connected either in a straight line, or at a
90° angle from another wall’s end
• Walls may not be placed to form a square or rectangle
that can’t be entered (must have a hatchway to form a
square or rectangle).
Once all walls have been placed in all table squares, roll one die for
each hatchway. On a 1-3 it starts closed, and on a 4-6 it starts open.
INTERIOR FIGHTING
When playing Boarding Actions, the game is lightly modified with
all of these changes:
• Objectives can only be seized if a line can be drawn
from the objective to the unit without passing
through a wall or closed hatchway.
• Units with Aircraft or similar rules can’t be used.
• Units with Ambush or similar rules count as having
the Scout rule instead.
• Units with Flying or similar rules count as having the
Strider rule instead.
• Weapons with Indirect or similar rules count as
ignoring cover instead.
HATCHWAYS
Hatchways can be in three states:
• Open - Units can see and move through.
• Closed - Units can’t see or move through.
• Destroyed - Counts as open and can’t be closed.
Models may not stand either fully or partly in a Hatchway, and units
with multiple models must always have all of their models on
either side of a Hatchway.
Operating Hatchways
There are two ways to operate a hatchway:
• At the beginning of a unit’s activation, it may open or close
one hatchway within 1”. If the unit successfully operates the
hatchway, it gets -1 to hit rolls until the end of the round.
• At the end of a unit’s activation, it may open or close one
hatchway within 1”.
If an enemy unit is within 1” of a hatchway that the player is trying
to operate, it may roll one die, and on a 4+ it blocks the unit from
opening or closing that hatchway.
Destroying Hatchways
All Hatchways count as having Defense 2+ and Tough(3), and may
be targeted for all types of attacks as if they were any other unit.
Once a Hatchway is destroyed, it permanently counts as open, and
can’t be closed anymore. If a unit is within 1” of a Hatchway when it
is destroyed, it takes D3+1 hits.
BOARDING ACTIONS GEAR
Before the game begins, each army may provide each of its Heroes
with a total of D3 pieces of gear, but it can’t have a total of more
than 3 pieces of gear for every 1000pts in the army.
Breaching Hammer: The Hero doesn’t get -1 to hit rolls when
operating a hatchway at the beginning of its activation.
Demolition Charge: Once per game, at the end of the Hero’s
activation, it may destroy one Hatchway within 3”.
Hatch Lock: Once per game, at the end of the Hero’s activation, it
may place a lock on one closed Hatchway within 1”. Enemy units
must roll one die when trying to open a locked Hatchway, on a 5+
the lock is removed and it is opened, else it remains closed.
Personal Teleporter: Once per game, when the Hero is activated,
you may place it anywhere within 6" of its position.
Tactical Radar: The Hero may seize an objective marker even if a
line can’t be drawn from the objective without passing through a
wall of closed hatchway.
