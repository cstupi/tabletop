# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 22

STRATEGIC DEPLOYMENT STYLES
These styles are meant to provide a balanced experience, with
interesting twists that require strategic thinking.
To select a random deployment style, simply roll one die:
1. No Man’s Land
2. Long Haul
3. Flank Assault
4. Frontal Clash
5. Tactical Push
6. Meeting Engagement
1. NO MAN’S LAND
DIAGRAMS: measured on the page images' 6" grid (72"×48" = 12×8 squares; the diagrams carry no inch labels); landscape table, long edges top and bottom.
Diagram — No Man's Land: zone A is a 6" strip along the top long edge; zone B is a 6" strip along the bottom long edge.
2. LONG HAUL
Diagram — Long Haul: zone A is a 12" strip along the left short edge; zone B is a 12" strip along the right short edge.
3. FLANK ASSAULT
Diagram — Flank Assault: zone B is a 12" strip along the bottom edge plus a 12"-wide block at the left end rising a further 12" above the strip (the strip to the right of the block is 60" long); zone A is the same shape rotated 180° (12" strip along the top edge, 12"-wide block at the right end hanging down a further 12").
4. FRONTAL CLASH
Diagram — Frontal Clash: zone B is a 6" strip along the bottom edge plus a central 24"-wide bump rising a further 6" (24" from the left edge to the bump, 24" from the bump to the right edge); zone A is its mirror along the top edge.
5. TACTICAL PUSH
Diagram — Tactical Push: zone A is an 18" strip along the left short edge plus a central bump 6" further in (24" from the left edge) and 24" tall, 12" from the top and from the bottom edge; zone B is its mirror along the right short edge. 3.5.1 (p.23) drew the bump 16" tall, 16" from the bottom edge; the strip depth and the bump's 6" depth are unchanged.
6. MEETING ENGAGEMENT
Diagram — Meeting Engagement: four corner rectangles, each 24" along the long edge by 12" along the short edge; zone A is the two top corners, zone B the two bottom corners.
