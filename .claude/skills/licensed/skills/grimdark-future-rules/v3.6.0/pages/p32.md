# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 32

USING ARMY TERRAIN
Players can choose to play using army terrain, which allows each
army to place a special piece of terrain that affects the game in
different interesting ways.
Before the game begins, each army either picks one army terrain,
or rolls to get one randomly.
To get army terrain randomly, first, roll one die to get one type of
Battle Terrain:
1. Army Banner
2. Battle Forge
3. Defensive Barrier
4. Icon of War
5. Protective Dome
6. Watch Tower
Then, roll one die to get one type of Relic Terrain:
1. Arcane Pillars
2. Enchanted Shrine
3. Faction Relic
4. Healing Well
5. Magic Armory
6. Twin Jumpgates
Finally, secretly pick one of the two results to be your army terrain
for the match.
Army terrain pieces should be somewhere between 2”x2” and
4”x4” in size, and somewhere between 2” and 4” tall. Each piece
counts as Impassable and Sight Blocking terrain.
During deployment, army terrain pieces are placed as if they were a
regular unit, but they can’t be placed within 6” of any objective
marker, or the table edges.
BATTLE TERRAIN
Army Banner
When a friendly unit activates within 6” of this army terrain, it gets
+2” when using Advance actions, and +4” when using Rush/Charge
actions.
Battle Forge
When a friendly unit activates within 6” of this army terrain, it gets
+1 to hit rolls when shooting.
Defensive Barrier
The area within 6” of this faction terrain counts as Difficult terrain
for enemy units.
Icon of War
When a friendly unit activates within 12” of this army terrain, it gets
+1 to hit rolls in melee when charging.
Protective Dome
The area within 6” of this faction terrain counts as Cover terrain for
friendly units.
Watch Tower
The area within 6” of this faction terrain counts as Dangerous
terrain for enemy units.
RELIC TERRAIN
Arcane Pillars
When placing this army terrain, place two copies of it within 12” of
each other (instead of placing just one).
Draw a straight line from one copy of this army terrain to the other.
When an enemy unit ends its activation with at least one model
touching the line, the unit takes D3 wounds.
Enchanted Shrine
When a friendly Caster activates within 6” of this army terrain, it
gets 1 spell token.
Faction Relic
When a friendly unit within 12” of this army terrain takes a Morale
Test, it gets +1 to its Morale Test roll.
Healing Well
When a friendly unit within 6” of this army terrain is activated, it
may restore D3 models/wounds. Note that new models may only
be restored if they can be placed in coherency with non-restored
models.
Magic Armory
When a friendly unit within 6” of this army terrain takes hits, it gets
+1 to its Defense rolls.
Twin Jumpgates
When placing this army terrain, place two copies of it within 24” of
each other (instead of placing just one).
When a friendly unit ends its activation within 3” of one of the
copies of this army terrain, you may place it anywhere within 3” of
the other copy of this army terrain.
