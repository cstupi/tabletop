# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 14

Cumbersome
May only use Hold or Advance actions, but can’t shoot when
moving.
Deadly(X)
Assign each wound to one model, and multiply it by X. Hits from
Deadly must be resolved first, and these wounds don’t carry over to
other models if the original target is killed.
Fast
Models with this special rule move +2” when using Advance and +4”
when using Rush/Charge.
Fear(X)
When in melee with this model, enemies count as having taken +X
wounds when checking who won.
Fearless
When a unit where most models have this rule fails a Morale Test,
roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
Flying
May move through units and terrain, and ignores the effects of
Difficult and Dangerous terrain.
Furious
When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only
the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
Example: A Furious model with a weapon with 1 Attack and Rending
scores one hit on a roll of 6, so it deals two hits, of which only the first
counts as having AP(+4) because of Rending.
Hero
Heroes with up to Tough(6) may deploy as part of one multi-model
unit without another Hero, but must use the unit’s Defense until all
other models have been killed. The Hero’s unit, and units within 6”
of friendly Heroes with Tough(9) or higher, may use the Hero’s
Quality for Morale Tests as long as the Hero isn’t Shaken.
Note that when a Hero joins a unit, they count as part of that unit,
so the unit’s size is increased by 1, and even if the Hero is the last
model remaining, it takes Morale Tests as the unit. This also means
that a Hero may never leave its unit or join another one.
Example: A Hero joins a unit of [5] models, so the unit’s size is [6].
Once all other models have been killed, the Hero must take a Morale
Test when it takes wounds, and if it fails a Morale Test in melee it will
Rout, because it’s a unit with half or less of its starting size.
Immobile
Models with this special rule may only use Hold actions.
Impact(X)
When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this
unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
Indirect
When using Hold actions and shooting, may target enemies that
are not in line of sight as if in line of sight, and ignores cover from
Sight Blockers.
Limited
Weapons with this rule may only be used once per game.
Regeneration
When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll
one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
Relentless
When this model shoots at enemies over 9” away, unmodified
result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6
for special rules).
Note: See examples from the Furious special rule for clarifications.
Reliable
Models attacks at Quality 2+ with this weapon.
Note that Reliable only changes the Quality value, so the roll can
still be modified, Fatigue still applies, etc.
Rending
Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those
hits get AP(+4).
