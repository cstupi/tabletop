# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 18

USING EXTRA ACTIONS
Before the game, players can agree to use any or all of the
following extra actions in the game:
• Last Stand
• Hunker Down
• Defensive Stance
• Focused Fire
• Heavy Charge
• Stealth Move
• Covering Fire
• Overwatch
When using these extra actions the game is played normally,
however players may pick any of the selected extra actions in
addition to the regular ones.
LAST STAND
Units may use a Last Stand action even if they are Shaken.
When using Last Stand actions, units may use a Hold, Advance,
Rush, or Charge action, but count as being Shaken for the purpose
of special rules until the beginning of their next activation.
Units in Last Stand get -1 to Quality and Defense rolls, halve their
movement, can’t contest or seize objectives, and can’t use any
special rules that require picking a target (ex.: Caster).
HUNKER DOWN
When using a Hunker Down action, the unit uses a Hold action and
remains idle, and it gets +1 to Defense rolls when taking hits from
shooting until its next activation.
Shaken units may use Hunker Down actions to get this bonus as
well as stopping to be Shaken.
DEFENSIVE STANCE
When using a Defensive Stance action, the unit uses a Hold action,
remains idle, and may not strike back until its next activation, and
it gets +1 to Defense rolls when taking hits in melee until its next
activation.
Shaken units may use Defensive Stance actions to get this bonus as
well as stopping to be Shaken.
FOCUSED FIRE
When using a Focused Fire action, the unit uses a Hold action, and
gets +1 to hit rolls when shooting at targets within 12”.
HEAVY CHARGE
When using a Heavy Charge action, the unit uses a Charge action to
charge a target that is between 3” and 6” away, and gets +1 to hit
rolls in melee.
STEALTH MOVE
When using a Stealth Move action, the unit uses an Advance action,
but may not shoot, and enemies shooting at it from over 12” away
get -1 to their hit rolls until its next activation.
COVERING FIRE
When using a Covering Fire action, the unit uses a Hold action and
shoots, but only hits on unmodified results of 6. Then, roll one die
per hit, and if you roll at least one 4+, then the target must take a
Morale Test (regardless of casualties). All hits are then discarded as
misses.
OVERWATCH
When using an Overwatch action, the unit uses a Hold action and
remains idle, and until its next activation it may react once to an
enemy unit’s activation at any point as that enemy unit moves or
shoots.
The unit may react to two things:
• Movement: Shoot at an enemy at any point during its
movement, but gets -1 to hit rolls.
• Shooting: Shoot back at an enemy within 24” that shot
at it, but gets -1 to hit rolls.
