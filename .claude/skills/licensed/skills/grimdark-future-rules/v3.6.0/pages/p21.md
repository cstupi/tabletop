# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 21

STANDARD DEPLOYMENT STYLES
These styles are meant to provide a balanced experience, with
fairly standard gameplay.
To select a random deployment style, simply roll one die:
1. Frontline
2. Ground War
3. Side Battle
4. Disordered
5. Spearhead
6. Opposing Forces
1. FRONT LINE
Note that this is the basic deployment from the core rules.
DIAGRAMS: measured on the page images' 6" grid (72"×48" = 12×8 squares; the diagrams carry no inch labels); landscape table, long edges top and bottom.
Diagram — Front Line: zone A is a 12" strip along the top long edge; zone B is a 12" strip along the bottom long edge.
2. GROUND WAR
Diagram — Ground War: zone A is a 24" strip along the left short edge; zone B is a 24" strip along the right short edge.
3. SIDE BATTLE
Diagram — Side Battle: zone A is the right triangle in the bottom-left corner with legs 36" up the left short edge and 48" along the bottom long edge; zone B is its mirror in the top-right corner (48" along the top edge, 36" down the right edge). The band between the two hypotenuses is 24" wide, measured perpendicular to them. 3.5.1 (p.22) drew the legs 30" up the short edge and 54" along the long edge.
4. DISORDERED
Diagram — Disordered: a central diamond of open ground with its vertices on the midpoints of the four edges (36" from the centre to the left and right vertices, 24" to the top and bottom vertices); zone A is the two top-corner triangles, zone B the two bottom-corner triangles.
5. SPEARHEAD
Diagram — Spearhead: zone A is the triangle on the left edge with its base the whole left edge and its apex 24" in from the left edge at mid-height (24" from the bottom edge); zone B is its mirror on the right edge, apex 24" in from the right edge; 24" between the two apexes.
6. OPPOSING FORCES
Diagram — Opposing Forces: zone A is a 24"×24" square in the top-left corner; zone B is a 24"×24" square in the bottom-right corner; 24" horizontal gap between them.
