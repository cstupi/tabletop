# Grimdark Future v3.6.0 — page 62

3. PLAY MISSION
After the new wave of enemies has been deployed, the game is
played normally.
Objectives: At the end of each round, if any enemy unit is within 3”
of an objective marker while defenders aren’t, then they seize the
objective and place one damage token on it. Once an objective has
taken 3 damage tokens, it is destroyed and removed from the
table.
Earning XP: Defending units earn XP in battle for each of the
following things:
• +3 XP: Killed a unit
• +5 XP: Killed a Hero unit
• +8 XP: Killed a Hero and its unit
Levelling Up: When a unit earns 5 XP you must roll one die to see
what trait it gets:
Result Trait
1 Agile - Moves +1” on Advance and +2” on
Rush/Charge actions.
2 Headstrong - Gets +1 to rolls when taking Morale
Tests.
3 Specialist - Gets +1 to hit rolls when in melee or
when shooting (pick one).
4 Resilient - Gets +1 to Defense rolls when blocking
hits.
5 Elite - May re-roll one roll of any kind once per
game.
6 Fast Learner - May pick any other trait from the list.
Each trait (except for Specialist) can only be gained once, so if you
roll a trait you already have keep re-rolling until you get a new trait
you don’t have yet. Units can’t earn more than 30xp, so they can
level up max. 6 times.
4. END OF ROUND
Point Rewards: At the end of rounds 3, 6, 9, and 12 if any
defending unit is within 3” of an objective marker while enemies
aren’t, then that objective counts as seized. The defending side
gets 40pts for each seized objective, plus 20pts if the defenders
seized the priority objective.
Buying Upgrades: Defenders may spend points to buy upgrades
for units that are within 9” of a seized objective. When buying
upgrades worth less than 0pts, you gain those points instead of
spending those points.
Reinforcing Units: Defenders may spend points to reinforce units
that are down to half or less of their total size or Tough value (for
single-model units) and that are within 9” of a seized objective.
Spend half as many points as the unit’s total point value (rounding
up), and then restore up to half of its total models or wounds (for
single-model units).
Recruiting Units: Defenders may spend points to recruit new units,
which must then be deployed fully within 9” of a seized objective.
Start Over: After players are done buying upgrades and recruiting
units, the round ends and a new one begins.
