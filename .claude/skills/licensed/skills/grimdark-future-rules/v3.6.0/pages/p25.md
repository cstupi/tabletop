PROGRESSIVE SCORING MISSIONS
1. Pitched Battle
The players must set up a total of D3+2 objective markers.
At the end of each round players get X VP for each objective marker
they control, where X is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
2. Domination
The players must set up a total of 4 objective markers.
Divide the non-deployment zone area of the table into 4 equal
quarters, and place one marker at the center of each.
At the end of each round players get X VP for each objective marker
they control, where X is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
3. Capture & Hold
The players must set up a total of 3 objective markers.
If a unit seizes a marker, remove it from the table, and it counts as
being carried by the unit. If the unit is Shaken or destroyed at any
point, the marker is dropped within 1” (placed by the opponent).
At the end of each round players get X VP for each objective marker
they control, where X is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
4. Demolition
The players must set up 1 objective marker each on the edge of
their deployment zone, over 9” away from the table edge.
Each objective marker belongs to the player that placed it, and if at
any point a unit seizes the enemy objective marker, then the
marker is destroyed and removed from play.
At the end of each round players get X VP if their objective has not
been destroyed, and X VP on the round in which they destroy the
enemy objective, where X is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
5. Headquarters
The players must set up 2 objective markers each.
The objectives must be placed within their deployment zone, over
9” away from the deployment zone’s edge and over 9” away from
their other objective.
At the end of each round players get X VP for each objective marker
they control, where X is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
6. Mosh Pit
The players must set up only 1 objective marker.
The objective marker must be placed over 9” away from the
deployment zones and the table edges.
At the end of each round players get X VP if they control the
objective marker, plus X VP if they were the first to seize it, where X
is the current round number.
After 4 rounds have been played the game ends, and the player
that scored most VPs wins.
