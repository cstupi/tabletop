# server/game

`Tabletop.GameServer` (`net10.0`, ASP.NET Core): the WebSocket game server. Clients, `sim/` and `viewer/` connect to `/ws`.
It reads management's rows, owns each game's append-only event log, and hosts the rules engine.

## Layout

- `Program.cs`: DI, `StartupGuard.Enforce`, `PropertiesSeeder` at startup (not in the `Test` environment), `/ws` (permessage-deflate with context takeover when the client offers it; `docs/architecture.md` → Transport), `/healthz`, listens on `127.0.0.1:5200` (`appsettings.json`; `Kestrel__Endpoints__Http__Url` overrides).

## How it works

- A threshold read from a stat carries its basis: `StatReader.TargetNumber` returns a `TargetRead` (value, printed value, each replace_stat and modify_stat row), `StepContext.Plan` puts its `ThresholdBasis` on the `RollPlan` and `Roller` on the `RollRecord`. `StatReader.Peek` reads without spending or recording, for a hit pool's basis in `roll-to-hit` and, under 3.6.0 p.14 (`GfEdition.HeroAuraTough`), the Quality a friendly Hero with Tough(9)+ in another non-Shaken unit within 6" lends to a morale test (`GfRolls.LentQualities`, read off the Hero's own unit's live set). Casting's 4+, fatigue and a property's own roll carry none.
- What the combat log reads off a roll (combat-log spec → What the server adds): `RollEffects.Attributed` sets `RollModifier.Via` from `LiveSet.GrantorOf` (pass one's `GrantedBy`, or the per-die grantor `LiveSet.WithGranted` stamps as `LiveProperty.Via`); `roll-to-hit`, a spell's `hit` and `roll-to-block` (the defender's side) pass `RollEffects.Triggers` (pure; `attacks: false` on the dice extra attacks added, which add none of their own) to `StepContext.Roll`, which asks it once per face into `RollRecord.FaceTriggers` and stamps `Die.Triggered` on the record's dice and a forced reroll's; `RulesEngine` stamps a die `SetDice` sets from it (`RollRecord.Stamp`); `GfRolls.Cover` counts p.11's majority, `GfAttack.CoverOf` turns it into a `CoverVerdict` (Indirect's dropped sight half as `IgnoredBy`; `GfAttack.IndirectApplies` gates Indirect's sight and cover halves, under 3.6 only on a Hold action, 3.6.0 p.14), `fire-groups`/`determine-attacks` settle it as `in_cover`, `cover_models`, `cover_of`, `cover_indirect`, and `roll-to-block` hands the one verdict to every group's `GfRolls.DefensePlan`, which names a cover suppressor (`LiveSet.Suppressors`) as `IgnoredBy`; `RollPlan.Cover` reaches `RollRecord.Cover` through `Roller`. Melee and spell hits carry none. A fatigued 3.6 hit plan (`GfRolls.HitPlan`, `GfEdition.FatiguedSixesTrigger` false) carries `HitsTriggerNothing`, which `Roller` copies onto the `RollRecord`: `roll-to-hit` names no face trigger and adds no attack for its successes, and `score-hits` reads no extra hit or grant off them (a failed die's rows, Hazardous's wound, still act).
- What the detailed log reads (detailed-log spec): `RollEffects.Attributed` writes `RollPlan.Ignored` (to `RollRecord.Ignored`): the modify_roll rows of properties pass one removed whose conditions hold (`LiveSet.RemovedRows`) and the negative hit modifiers a `to_hit_penalties` suppression drops (a `foreign_to_hit_penalties` one keeps those `LiveSet.OwnedBy` the roller), and the positive modifiers of `IGameRules.DefenseRoll` a `positive_defense` suppression drops, cover's +1 among them, `By` the suppressors; `StatReader` writes `ThresholdBasis.Ignored` the same way for replace_stat and modify_stat rows; `LiveSet.ReadRemoved` records each removed rule it reads into `AppliedEntries.Ignore`, which `SequenceInterpreter` writes as `StepCompleted.Ignored`; `StepContext.Roll` stamps `DieReplacement.By` with the rule whose force_reroll row matched the die. None of it changes a result.
- A once grant a step reads is spent through `StepContext.Consume`: outside a sequence `IGameRules.HoldsOnceGrants` names it is `RollEffects.Consume` at once; under one (Grimdark Future: `attack-sequence`, `spell-hits`, `rule-hits`, `GfAttack.IsAttackRoot`) the step emits nothing and writes `held_<unit>_<grant>` into its `StepCompleted.Values`, and `attack-end` (`GfAttack.AttackEndStep`, or the step that finishes the frame early) releases every grant held by the frame and the frames under it by Consume's rule (`RollEffects.Release`), so every row of a once entry applies across the attack. `GfMorale`'s `test` keeps its own `held_` keys for `override`.
- An `optional` add_hit attacks row (Predator: "may roll +1 attack") is the roller's choice: `roll-to-hit` rolls its pools, writes `offer_<pool>_q<quality>` for the attacks optional rows set off and completes with Repeat; the repeat asks the roller's team `extra_attacks` (Options Yes and No, pass is No) and on Yes rolls each pool's added dice at its Quality (`attacks: false`) and records the rows as applied. Mandatory rows add as they fire; `RollEffects.PerDie` returns the two apart and records no optional row.
- `GfAttack.IndirectApplies` reads Quick Throw by name: under 3.6 Indirect's sight and cover halves apply on a Hold, or on any action when an attacking model holds Quick Throw; wherever Indirect is recorded off a Hold (`GfAttack.RecordIndirect`), Quick Throw is recorded with it.
- `/ws` accepts a socket (`/ws?watch=1`: read-only, below); `ConnectionHandshake` reads the first frame, which must be `Connect` (8 KB, within `HandshakeTimeout`). Later frames: 64 KB, a `ReplaceDraft` 512 KB, presence 16 KB (1009 over). A frame with a control or bidi-override character in any string is refused by its command or edit id (`InboundText`). `FrameLimiter` holds commands, edits and sight queries to 20/s (burst 40; the read loop waits; dev mode admits them at machine speed, `GameServerOptions.MachineSpeed`, as `GameServerFactory` does, unless `TABLETOP_FRAME_RATE` is set) and drops pings over 3/s and presence over 30/s. An account holds at most 4 sockets (the oldest closed 1000); a socket closes 1008 at its JWT's `exp`, which a `Reauth` with a refreshed token of the same account moves on once connect's authorization holds again (still this participant; a watcher still an admin and the game still there); a token that does not verify or names another account is `Refused(ReauthId, NotPermitted)`, and one whose account no longer holds the socket is refused and closes it 1008; a replaced socket's read loop stops. Every figure: design doc → Limits.
- Handshake order: client version against `MinimumClientVersion` (`Rejected(UpgradeRequired)`), `ISessionVerifier` (`Rejected(InvalidSession)`), `IGameRows.FindParticipantAsync` (`Rejected(NotAParticipant)` unless the session is an admin and the game exists), `SessionRegistry.GetOrHydrateAsync`, `GameSession.ConnectAsync` (`WatchAsync` for an admin watcher and for any socket opened as `/ws?watch=1`), then the read loop.
- A read-only socket (`/ws?watch=1`, the client's replay) admits whoever connect admits, a participant or an admin, in any status, `ended` included, and holds it as a watcher: `Welcome.ParticipantId` `Guid.Empty`, everything it sends refused as below. Its reauth checks the account is still that participant (an admin watcher: still an admin).
- An admin watcher (session `roles` claim holds `admin`, no participant row) gets what a teamless participant gets, with `Welcome.ParticipantId` `Guid.Empty`. `GameSession` holds watchers in `_watchers`, apart from `_clients`, so lobby, presence and Asks never meet one. A watcher's sight query is answered; its command, edit, presence or ping is `Refused` with `RefusalReason.Watcher` and nothing else. From `playing` on a participant whose account holds no team is answered the same way (`GameSession.Watches`), except while deployment waits for players, and `CommandDispatcher.Dispatch` refuses every command from a teamless actor.
- A verifier or database exception closes the socket 1011; it is not a rejection.
- A handshake close (`ConnectionHandshake.CloseAsync`, after a `Rejected` too) waits up to 2 s for the peer's close frame before the socket is disposed: Node's WebSocket inflates a compressed frame asynchronously and drops it when the connection ends first.
- `StytchJwtVerifier` checks RS256 against Stytch's JWKS, issuer `stytch.com/{project}`, audience the project id, and the top-level `account_id` claim (our account UUID). The `roles` claim (Stytch role ids) becomes `VerifiedSession.Roles`; absent is none. An unknown `kid` asks `IJwksSource.RefreshAsync`.
- `Hydrator` builds a `GameSession` from the rows, the `game.game_event` log and `GameRulesRegistry.Resolve(info.GameRules)`. The engine comes from `IRulesEngineFactory.Create(rules, info)`.
- A game that has not ended runs its game rules' current set (games-follow-the-current-rules spec): `PropertiesSeeder` records each seeded hash in `CurrentProperties`, and `Hydrator` moves a game on another hash to it (`IGameRows.RepinPropertiesAsync`, one Information log line) before anything reads `info`. A loaded `PropertySet` aliases the effect rows of the other sets of its game rules by entry, position path and kind (`EffectAliases`), and `EffectById` falls back to them, so a grant minted under the old set acts. An ended game keeps its set.
- `GameSession` is one game in memory. All mutation runs on its mailbox loop (a `Channel`), so there is no lock. An account may hold several sockets on a game (`AccountSockets` closes its oldest past 4).
- A command frame goes to `CommandDispatcher.Dispatch`, which returns `Applied(events, warning)` or `Refused`. It never appends or broadcasts.
- `Classify` sorts commands: `Override` (menu edits: moves, markers, wounds, conditions, score, round), `Undo`, `Abandon`, `Answer`, `Engine` (`SetDice`).
- Dice and modifiers are the rules' alone: no player command rerolls, sets a face or adds a modifier. `SetDice` is the rules
  suite's way to fix a roll's faces. `RulesEngine` takes it only when built with `acceptsSetDice`, which comes from
  `GameServerOptions.AcceptsSetDice`: on in dev mode (`StartupGuard.DevMode`; the compose `game` service, which the suite's socket
  driver targets), off otherwise. The sim's in-process driver and `GameServerFactory` turn it on. Off, `SetDice` is refused under
  every enforcement setting (`Judge` says `Illegal`, `Execute` refuses `NotPermitted`).
- `GameSession.RunToRestAsync` is the write path for a command, the catch-up at start and the end of setup: the command's events, then `IRulesEngine.Advance` repeatedly until it returns nothing (cap 10000 iterations), each batch applied to the projection as it comes; then one `IEventLog.AppendAsync` of every batch (one transaction, one `NpgsqlBatch`), then each batch broadcast as its own `EventsMessage`, in order. Nothing is sent before the append returns.
- A failed append faults the session before anything is sent; the projection, ahead of the log, is discarded with it. An `Advance` exception or a batch the projection cannot apply rolls the run back: nothing stored or sent, `Play` rebuilt from the stored log, the command `Refused(Unknown)` with a fixed detail. A log at `MaxEventsPerGame` refuses every command.
- A fault closes every socket 1011. Close reasons are fixed phrases cut to 123 bytes; exception text goes to the log only. Close reasons are fixed phrases cut to 123 bytes; exception text goes to the log only.
- `RulesEngine.Advance` is `SequenceInterpreter.Advance` while frames are on the stack; the `IScheduler` runs only when no framed Ask is open and the stack is empty. While a framed Ask is open it emits only `SequenceInterpreter.StaleGates`.
- A `GameEndingBreach` from `IGameRules.JudgeAdvance` (Grimdark Future: `mission_length`, round 5 about to start) ends the game under `strict`, passes quietly under `silent`, and under `warn` is `OfferEnd`: `GameOverAsk` opens one sealed, unframed `game_over` Ask (End, Continue) to every team with a participant, once per round boundary. Any End closes it at once (`GameOverAsk.Settle`) and the next `Advance` writes the `strict` `game_ended`; every team's Continue lets `round_started` through with the warning. A `set_round` while it is open closes it unanswered.
- Undo is `Undone(toSeq)`: events are never removed. `EventApplier.EffectiveLog` is the live log; `Replay.To` rebuilds a projection to a seq.
- Hydration decides from the log, not the row: an ended log over a `playing` row writes the row and advances nothing; any other log catches up through `RunToRestAsync`.
- Management's join and leave write participant rows without telling the session. In lobby and setup the session reads the rows again
  (`RefreshInfoAsync`, a `GameInfo` broadcast only when they differ) on every connect and when a participant's last socket closes (the
  client's Leave: `POST /leave`, then the socket), and `EndSetup` reads them before it snapshots, so a player who left brings no army.
- Lobby authority: `SetConfiguration`, `StartSetup` and `EndSetup` are the creator's. `PickTeam` takes an empty team; a team another account holds is joined only by the creator placing them (`PickTeam(TeamId, ParticipantId)`). `PickArmy` and `EndSetup` check the army against `ArmyLimits`, and its edition against the game's (`ArmyLimits.EditionBreach`: `Refused(NotPermitted)` naming both editions, EndSetup's naming the participant; the waiting-for-players `PickArmy` too, through `PlayableArmyAsync`); a setup edit's asset ids must belong to a participant (`IGameRows.AssetOwnersAsync`); every setup edit's result is held to `DraftLimits` (removals always apply). A `SetConfiguration` naming another edition's hash re-resolves `Rules`, broadcasts `RulesOptionsMessage` (the new set's option catalog, `Zones` and `TerrainWords`, shaped as `Welcome`'s) before the `GameInfoMessage` (both clients rebuild option controls, zones and terrain words on the GameInfo), and clears armies of the other edition; its options are checked against the new edition's catalog.
- Self-play (game-flow spec → One account, several teams): a participant row is a seat, one team and one army; an account holds one row per team. `GameSession.SeatsOf`/`TeamsOf` read the connection's account through its row. `PickTeam` adds a team (`TakeTeamAsync`: the teamless row takes it, else `IGameRows.AddSeatAsync` with an id after the account's others, `SeatIdAfter`), `LeaveTeam` drops one (`DropSeatAsync` keeps the oldest row), `PickArmy` names the team. `ActorFor` picks the seat a command acts as: the team `AnswerAsk.TeamId` names, else the first unanswered team of mine the Ask addresses; a `MoveModels` the team of the model it moves. `EventFilter.ForViewer` takes the viewer's set of teams.
- Team games (game-flow spec → Participants; rulebook p.50; owner ruling: "Either player from the team can take the activation across any unit in their team."): an Ask names teams only; any seat of an addressed team answers it, for any unit of the team, and any seat places the team's deploy pick (`HandMoves`). The dispatcher refuses only a seat of a team the Ask does not address, or a team's second answer. `Participant.ArmyName` comes from the army row, else the snapshot.
- Waiting for players (game-flow spec): from the end of setup, while the log is empty and `WaitingForPlayers.Holds` (a team with no participant, or no army with a unit on it), `Advance` runs no engine and commands are refused `WrongStatus`; `HandleWaitingEditAsync` accepts only `PickTeam` onto a team no one holds and `PickArmy` for a seat of mine with no army on a waiting team, which `JoinArmyAsync` snapshots (`IGameRows.JoinArmyAsync`), adds to the board in participant-id order and follows with the engine run. A teamless participant is no watcher while it waits.
- Rows written: in `lobby` and `setup`, teams, participant team/army and seat rows (`AddSeatAsync`, `RemoveSeatAsync`), configuration, `StartSetupAsync` (draft tree, status to `setup`), `WriteDraftAsync` (one write per drop), `EndSetupAsync` (final tree, `army_snapshot` per participant, status to `playing`).
- From `playing` on, the row writes are a participant's team and army while deployment waits for players (`SetParticipantTeamAsync`, `AddSeatAsync`, `JoinArmyAsync`) and `EndGameAsync` (`playing` to `ended`, guarded on `playing`), after the log ends the game. A failed write is logged and the next hydration retries it.
- The engine holds no state between calls. A step reads position, values and history from the log (`FrameReader`) and returns one outcome: `Complete`, `Finish`, `OpenAsks`, or `Push`.
- `RulesEngineFactory` loads the game's `PropertySet` by `game.PropertiesHash` (cached per hash) and calls `ISchedulerFactory`. A missing hash throws `PropertiesNotFoundException`.
- `SchedulerFactory` picks `AlternatingActivationScheduler` or `PlayerTurnScheduler` from `game.SchedulerShape`.
- Dice: die `i` of a game is a pure function of (`rng_seed`, `i`) in `DiceSource.Draw`. `DiceCursor.DrawnSoFar` counts every die in the whole log, undone events included (roll records plus rerolls); edits draw nothing.
- Effects run two passes over live properties. Pass one (`LiveProperties`, to a fixed point): which properties are live, grants, suppressions, parameter changes, suppressed modifier classes. Pass two (`RollEffects` and readers): what the live properties do to a roll or stat.
- `StepCompleted.Applied` lists the catalog entries that acted in that run of the step, each once, in order of first application;
  null when none did. Each `StepContext` owns an `AppliedEntries`: a live set settled through `StepContext.Settle` records every
  effect row that applies, and a ruleset hook that changes a result adds the rule it names (`ctx.Applied.Add`). A rule that acts in
  the scheduler or in action legality (Scout, Ambush, Immobile) completes no step and is not listed; Aircraft's action restriction is
  not listed either, but its move (p.13: +30", no terrain) is, on the move step. The rules suite reads it
  to judge which rules fired.
- `SequenceInterpreter` opens a "Continue: {step}" confirm Ask between steps, and before a repeat of a frame's first step, when `Info.WaitsBetweenSteps` is on, gated to the actor's team. The client never offers the flag; it serves development. Under the same flag a frame with no actor and no parent (the roll-off) is gated to every team; without it the roll-off rolls and ranks in one run (roll-off result spec). A frame waiting only on a Confirm the flag no longer asks for (a log stopped at the roll-off's gate when it opened whatever the flag said) has it closed unanswered (`SequenceInterpreter.StaleGates`, asked by `RulesEngine.Advance` while a framed Ask is open). The roll-off's ranking step writes `place_1..place_n` and `rolled_<ordinal>_<k>`, each team's faces, which the client's `RollOffBanners` shows.
- An override command passes `MenuLimits` first: an action, or a grant's entry and effect, named in the property set; a condition 1–64 characters (free names by design); positions finite within the table's diagonal of its centre; bounded numbers.
- An override command passes `MenuLimits` first: an action, or a grant's entry and effect, named in the property set; a condition 1–64 characters (free names by design); positions finite within the table's diagonal of its centre; bounded numbers.
- A `MoveModels` goes through `HandMoves.Judge` and `EnforcementPolicy.Decide`: placing the unit the actor's team last picked at a deploy Ask from the shelf onto the table, a shelved unit of the actor's team inside a container the rules allow (`IGameRules.MayDeployInside`; Grimdark Future, p.15: a Transport of its team being placed now or never deployed, room by p.15's spaces, set aside exactly when the Transport is; no Ask is answered, so no deployment turn is spent), or back out of one still on the shelf, is legal (passengers spec); placing a Transport whose occupants the rules no longer allow is not; anything else is `hand_move`, which `silent` accepts, `warn` warns and `strict` refuses. A placement on another model's base is
  `IGameRules.PlacementBreach`'s (Grimdark Future: `move_overlap`, `GfMoves.OverlapBreach`), legal or not, and so is a placement before round one outside the team's deployment zone when the game has zones (`deploy_zone`, `GrimdarkFutureRules.ZoneBreach`: p.6 fully within the zone, p.14 a Scout fully within 12" of it, 3.6.0 p.15 9", `GfEdition.ScoutReach`; Ambush is not zone-bound), and under 3.6 so is a Scout placed in the Scouts phase with any base within 9" of a model of an enemy unit that is not itself a Scout (`scout_distance`, `GrimdarkFutureRules.ScoutBreach`: 3.6.0 p.15 "over 9” away from non-Scout enemy units", `GfEdition.ScoutClearance`), and so is a set-aside unit placed from the shelf once rounds have begun (an Ambusher) with any base within 9" of an enemy model (`ambush_distance`, `GrimdarkFutureRules.AmbushBreach`: p.13 "anywhere over 9” away from enemy units"; `GfAmbush`'s Infiltrate 3", Repel Ambushers 12", Ambush Beacon's 6" exemption); it is appended to a `hand_move` breach's detail.
- Under 3.6 (`GfEdition.VanguardClearance` 9") a Fanatic or Vanguard placement (the rule's `reposition` frame's move Ask) with any base within 9" of a model of an enemy unit not carrying Vanguard is `vanguard_distance` (`GrimdarkFutureRules.VanguardBreach`, last in `AnswerBreach`'s `MoveRegion` chain: "over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units").
- An Ask answer inside its legal set is also asked of `IGameRules.AnswerBreach`, which names the rule broken; the dispatcher decides it as a
  `Breach` (`StrictRefuses(Illegal)`) under the enforcement setting. Grimdark Future's (`GfRules.Moves.cs`) are `move_not_offered`, `move_exit`,
  `move_off_table`, `move_impassable`, `move_allowance` (`GfMoves.PathBreach`, p.7 and p.11, against the Ask's own parameters, plus the landing
  against every impassable volume, which a Flying unit's Ask does not list, unless the region `IgnoresTerrain`: an Aircraft, p.13), `difficult_cap`
  (`GfMoves.DifficultCapBreach`: p.11 holds every model of a `MoveRegion` to its `DifficultCap` once any model's path, or an earlier segment of the
  move, enters difficult ground), `aircraft_minimum` (`GfMoves.MinimumBreach`: every model moves at least `MinDistance`, p.13) and p.7's contact clauses
  (`GfMoves.ContactBreach`: `move_overlap` on a base, `move_through_models` unless the region `PassesModels`, `move_separation` within the region's `Separation`),
  then p.7's `move_coherency` (`GfMoves.CoherencyBreach`: a unit in coherency before the answer and out of it after, `Coherency.Check(1, 3, 9)`),
  then p.9's `charge_contact` (`GfMoves.ChargeContactBreach`: a charge move, or its pass, that leaves no charging model within `GfNames.ChargeContactGrace`
  (0.5") of a `MoveRegion.ContactWith` model when the unit could reach contact in coherency: `UnitFormation.InContact` over the Ask's own parameters). All read the
  answer through `GfAnswers.Moves`, the reading the move steps apply, so a check never passes a relocation the step reads differently.
  A charge's reading is the charge's end (`GfMoves.ChargeLanding`; owner ruling: "charging base to base is TOO strict and requires literally overlapping. It
  should have a small grace window, and if it had the movement to make the charge then it should be accepted."): when the answer breaks none of
  `PathBreach`, `DifficultCapBreach`, `ContactBreach` and `CoherencyBreach`, each charging model ending within the grace of a target model is drawn
  straight in to touch it (`Collision.Abut`), and when none ends within it and none touches, the nearest one that can be is drawn in from where it
  ended; a model is drawn in only where all four still hold. Base contact is then true contact for the defender's close-in. A stop (`Continues`) or a
  disembark is read as it is. The charge Ask carries the grace as `MoveRegion.ContactGrace`, which both clients' landings read.
  Each move is judged along the shortest path its base can take (`Movement.Check`, `GfMoves.Route`; p.5): round the impassable volumes the Ask lists and
  the models the answer does not move, and round difficult and dangerous ground when that fits the allowance, so a Rush round a wood is not capped;
  `move_through_models` is a move that fits only through models. `ApplyMove` measures and tests dangerous and difficult terrain along the same path

## Conventions and pitfalls

- The engine never appends or broadcasts. The session does both.
- Dice are never drawn from a clock or a global generator. Anything random goes through `Roller` and `DiceCursor`. `Roller` clamps a roll to `MaxDice` (1000) with a logged warning and throws on a negative count.
- Content is one folder per edition (`Content/<GfEdition.ContentFolder>/`): `core.properties.json`, the hand parts, the generated part and `army-books/`. A new edition is a new folder, a new id in `RuleSets`, a `GfEdition` and a `GrimdarkFutureRules` instance. Editing a 3.5.1 file makes a new 3.5.1 hash; 3.6 files never move a 3.5.1 game. `ContentFoldersTests` pins the 3.5.1 hash; a deliberate 3.5.1 edit updates it.
- Authored properties are each folder's `core.properties.json`, every `*.part.json`, and every `army-books/*.book.json` of the same game rules. `PropertiesSeeder.Compose` folds them into one document per core file, ordered by name and uid. `PropertiesSeeder.EmbeddedPart(contentFolder, fileName)` reads one part.
- Seeding is by content hash at startup. Editing any of these files makes a new set with a new hash, and every game that has not ended moves to it at its next hydration; an ended game keeps its hash. Stored rows keep the document's order in `position` (actions, effects, conditions, condition values); a present set with null positions gets them from its document, ids kept.
- Two sources carrying an entry of the same name must have equal canonical forms, else `Compose` throws.
- A `*.book.json` is never edited by hand. Fix `scripts/ArmyBooks` and run `./scripts/army-books generate`.
- The 3.6 `army-rules.part.json` holds only names a 3.6.0 book describes or carries, each with that book's text (`Tabletop.ArmyBooks.Tests/ArmyRules36Tests`, over the 3.6 dump; its `AwaitingTheir36Text` lists the entries still on their 3.5.1 text and effects). A same-effect rename carries the entry to the 3.6 name; where the C# half reads the 3.5 name it reads both under either edition (`GfNames.IsStrafing` for Crossing Strike, `GfNames.IsHero` for Unique Hero), since no 3.5 book carries a 3.6 name. Counter in Melee (3.5 Counter-Attack) is read as Counter on the holding model's weapons, or every model's when granted to the unit (`GfMelee.StrikesFirst`).
- `army-book-rules.part.json` (`PropertiesSeeder.GeneratedPartName`) is never edited by hand. Authoring a name in the hand part needs `./scripts/army-books generate` before the server composes, or `Compose` refuses two entries of one name.
- New content files need the `EmbeddedResource` glob in `Tabletop.GameServer.csproj`; its patterns are `Content/*/*.properties.json`, `Content/*/*.part.json`, `Content/*/army-books/*.book.json`, and the logical name turns `%(RecursiveDir)`'s separators into dots.
- The game server writes a game's rows only in `lobby` or `setup`, a participant's team and army while deployment waits for players, `properties_hash` at hydration until the game ends, plus `playing` to `ended` once. Management writes at creation, end, leave and delete.
- `WaitsBetweenSteps` is the per-game flag behind every "Continue" Ask (`SequenceInterpreter.cs`). Players never see it.
- Every `IGameRules` wrapper that forwards by hand (the tests' `WithSequences`) forwards `MaxWounds`, `Shooters`, `MayDeployInside`, `DeployInside`, `MaySetAside`, `AnswerBreach`, `PlacementBreach`, `OptionCatalog`, `Zones`, `TerrainWords`, `OptionProblems` and `SightPolicy`, or a wrapped ruleset answers 1, nobody, never, nothing, nobody, nothing broken, no options, the default zone legs, no terrain words and core sight.
- `ProjectionDesyncException` at hydration is a server bug: every reconnect replays the same event.
- The first `Connect` frame has `HandshakeTimeout` (5 s by default).
- Schema changes are management migrations, never made here.
